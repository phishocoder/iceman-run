# Research Notes: Drake - "Janice STFU"

## Confirmed public context

- Apple Music lists "Janice STFU" as a Drake song on `ICEMAN`, dated May 15, 2026.
- Multiple music/news sources report `ICEMAN` as a May 15, 2026 Drake release and describe "Janice STFU" as one of the breakout tracks.
- Music coverage and fan/source aggregation identify the key sample/interpolation as Lykke Li's "I Follow Rivers."
- "I Follow Rivers" appears on Lykke Li's 2011 album `Wounded Rhymes`, which Apple Music dates to February 25, 2011.
- Drake and Lykke Li have prior creative history through "Little Bit" from the `So Far Gone` era, so the sample reads as a callback as well as a fresh flip.

## Creative interpretation used for the game

- Ice/cold imagery: `ICEMAN`, Toronto skyline, frozen stage, ice records, cold streaks.
- Noise/gossip pressure: "Janice" is treated as a symbolic chatter/gossip/media hazard, not a real named target.
- Sample lineage: the "follow" idea becomes lane-chasing enemies and collectible records, without copying the source song's melody or audio.
- Fan accessibility: one-screen arcade loop, immediate start, keyboard/touch controls, short runs, score and streak.
- Rollout lore: the Toronto ice-block release-date stunt became the central "melt the date" objective.
- Livestream/event lore: CN Tower and bot-farm imagery became checkpoints and hazard waves.
